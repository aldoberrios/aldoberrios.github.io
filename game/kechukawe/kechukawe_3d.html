How to play

Each territory begins with one occupied lof. Roll 1–4 to place that many pieces in empty positions in your territory. Once it is full, use remaining points to remove opponents’ pieces by selecting them. Rolling 5 wins immediately.

Click highlighted circles to spend each point. You can choose where to remove opposing pieces. Players who lose all their pieces after the opening round are eliminated. The last remaining player wins.

Source: Adapted from the Ngulumapu version described in Kechukawe (Scribd). The interactive turn handling and automatic opponents are modern design choices, not claims about historical practice.
